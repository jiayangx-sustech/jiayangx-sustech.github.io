Project 1

Fast 4D Spectral X-ray Tomography via Co-Designed Sparse-View Scans and Reconstruction

CUHK, SUSTech · 10/2025 – Present
Algorithm Lead · Advisors: Prof. Chao Wang and Prof. Jizhou Li

A conventional 3D chemical scan of a battery particle takes almost two hours and nearly 40,000 projections, and the particle can change during the scan. I redesigned the scan and the reconstruction together.

  • Co-Scan. Short runs of neighboring angles at each energy, spread over several rotations: the scan fell from 118 to 19 minutes.
  • Co-Recon. A physics-informed deep learning reconstruction from 2.5% of the projections; its chemical maps matched full-angle references (Pearson r above 0.90).
  • Changes during long scans. Across a two-hour conventional scan, one region’s crack fraction rose from 23.3% to 37.4%.

Outcome First-author manuscript · Nature Materials · In preparation

Project 2

Multi-Modal MRI Brain Tumor Segmentation Robust to Any Combination of Missing Modalities

SUSTech · 10/2025 – 04/2026
Undergraduate Researcher · Advisor: Prof. Chao Wang

One model segments brain tumors from any of the 15 combinations of four MRI sequences, restoring what is missing inside the network instead of synthesizing images.

  • Geometric scaffold. Fourier-encoded coordinates give the bottleneck a spatial reference that does not depend on which sequences are present.
  • Directional distillation. A teacher with all sequences aligns the student’s feature directions, weighted toward the tumor.
  • BraTS 2021. 87.00% Dice with all sequences, 77.01% on average with missing ones, about 19M parameters.

Outcome Sole-author paper · IEEE ICIP 2026 · Published

Project 3

Finite-Smoothing Efficiency and Inference in Single-Index Models

SUSTech · 10/2024 – 09/2026
Undergraduate Researcher · Advisor: Prof. Xin Chen

When the error is non-Gaussian and independent of the predictors, its distribution carries information about the single-index direction beyond the conditional mean. Single-Index Kernel Density Regression (SI-KDR) uses it by refitting the link at each candidate direction and scoring the residuals with a kernel likelihood.

  • Exact cost of smoothing. The leading covariance equals the conditional-mean bound times Λ/G, so SI-KDR beats the bound exactly when Λ < G (Gaussian design).
  • Tuning and tests. Covariance-trace bandwidths cut direction risk by 56–74%; score tests, exact under Gaussian design, gave 93.8–96.6% coverage.
  • High dimensions. Up to 10,000 predictors; the guarantees carry over when screening keeps every active predictor.

Outcome First-author manuscript · JASA · Under review · arXiv:2611.22384

Project 4

QuadFocusNet: Learning to Route Multi-Scale Patches for Multivariate Time Series Forecasting

SUSTech
Independent project

Instead of one fixed patch length, QuadFocusNet tokenizes a series at several scales and lets a learnable router weight them for each input.

  • Multi-scale routing. Parallel tokenizers with different patch lengths, mixed by weights computed from the whole series.
  • Two streams. A trend MLP and a seasonal Transformer with prompts; the forecast is a residual added to the last observed value.
  • Results. Lowest RMSE against PatchTST, iTransformer, Transformer, Bi-LSTM and TCN on six datasets.

Outcome Sole-author paper · IJCNN 2026 · Published (Oral)
